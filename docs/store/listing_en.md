# Store listing — English

**Name:** Tayf

**Short description:**
A calm puzzle on your table: every attempt leaves a ghost that repeats your steps and opens the way.

**Full description:**
Tayf (Arabic for "ghost") is a mixed-reality puzzle game whose rooms stand on a real table in front of you.

Each loop gives you a limited number of steps. When they run out you return to the start, and the path you walked becomes a blue ghost that repeats your moves exactly while you walk beside it. Doors open only while someone stands on their plate, so let your ghost hold the door while you cross to the star.

Every room makes you plan before you move: where your ghost should stop, and when you should arrive. Later come clock doors that close if you are late, striped plates that close their door instead of opening it, and rooms that need more than one ghost.

- Endless rooms, generated fresh each run
- Place, turn and resize the table anywhere in your room
- A hint, and a full solution to watch, when you are stuck
- End a loop whenever you like and turn your walk into a ghost with one press

**Features:**
- "Cooperate with yourself" puzzles: a simple idea with growing depth
- Mixed reality: the table sits in your real room
- Endless rooms
- Calm: no violence, no timer pressure

**Keywords:** puzzle, mixed reality, logic, table, calm, Arabic
